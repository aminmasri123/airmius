New in 1.0.32:

- Start membership applications directly from club profiles on web and mobile.
- Mobile club profiles now expose core web information such as description, admins, progress, badges and posts.
- Mobile club profiles now support messaging, following and blocking.

- Private profiles are no longer shown to unrelated people in global search.
- Clear, focused account registration without internal account-management options.
- Google and Outlook registration now preserve the selected account type.
- Password help, 2FA, email verification and account deletion appear only in their correct areas.
- Improved registration guidance in German, English, French and Arabic.
- Sport partner searches now show date and time clearly.
- City, postal code, venue and address can be entered separately.
- Sport partners can also be found by postal code and venue.
