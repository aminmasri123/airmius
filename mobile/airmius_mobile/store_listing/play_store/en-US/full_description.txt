Airmius brings sports clubs, teams, members and coaches together in one mobile platform.

Find clubs and teams, view club information, manage membership requests, follow training and event dates, stay in touch through messages and notifications, and manage your profile and privacy settings.

For clubs, Airmius provides mobile tools for member communication, membership requests, contribution information, documents, roles, teams and appointments. The app is designed to bring the Airmius web experience into a focused native Flutter app.

Key features:
- Find clubs and teams
- Start membership applications directly from a club profile
- View the same core club information available on the web
- Follow clubs, send a message and block clubs
- Receive club news and notifications
- Keep training and events in view
- Access invoices and payment status where enabled
- Manage documents and uploads where enabled
- Manage your profile, sports profile and privacy settings
- Use the app in German, English, French and Arabic

Available features may vary depending on the club, your role and the Airmius configuration enabled for your account.
